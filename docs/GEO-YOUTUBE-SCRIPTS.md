## Video 2: "How to Spot a Flooded Car (Red Flags You Can't Miss)"

**Length:** 3 minutes | **Topic:** Risk avoidance | **Goal:** Build authority + FAQ traffic

### Script

```
[INTRO — 0:00-0:15]
"I'm Freddie from Paqar. Today: how to spot a flooded car before you buy it.

Flood damage costs you RM2-5k in hidden repairs. This video could save you a fortune."

[HOOK — 0:15-0:45]
"Here's the scary part: a flooded car can look fine for the first 3 months.
Then the electrical system fails. Door locks stop working. Windows jam. Engine starts misfiring.

By then, you've already paid. Too late to walk away.

So today I'm showing you the signs you CAN see right now."

[SEGMENT 1: Physical Red Flags — 0:45-1:45]
"SIGN 1: Musty / Moldy Smell
Open the door. Smell the cabin. If it smells like old carpet or mold, that's water damage.
Don't ignore this. It's the #1 sign. Walk away immediately.

SIGN 2: Rust Under the Car
Get down and look at the undercarriage bolts. 
If the car is only 5 years old but the bolts are heavily rusted, that's flood damage.
Normal cars don't rust that fast underneath.

SIGN 3: Water Stains on Seats/Carpet
Check the bottom of seats. Look under floor mats.
Brownish stains that won't come off? That's floodwater.
Check the trunk too.

SIGN 4: Engine Oil That's Foamy
This is critical. Pop the hood. Check the dipstick.
If the oil looks foamy or milky instead of brown, water got into the engine.
That engine is dead. Don't buy it.

SIGN 5: Electrical Issues
Test everything:
- Power windows (all 4)
- Power locks
- Air conditioning
- Radio/stereo
- Dashboard lights

If things work one day and fail the next, that's water damage at work."

[SEGMENT 2: The Definitive Test — 1:45-2:30]
"Here's the thing: your eyes can lie. A flooded car cleaned up looks fine.

That's why you MUST get a JomCheck inspection (RM99, takes 1 hour).

JomCheck will:
- Check accident history (saw it in the system)

- Look for hidden rust in door panels
- Check engine compression (water damage shortens lifespan)
- Review service records (prove maintenance history)

This RM99 investment could save you RM5,000 in repairs.

Don't skip this. Ever."

[SEGMENT 3: Timeline — 2:30-2:50]
"One more thing: flood damage appears LATER.

Month 1: Car looks fine, runs fine.
Month 3: Door locks start failing (RM300 to fix).
Month 6: Electrical gremlins, engine issues.
Month 12: Rust spreads inside panels. Engine reliability drops.

By the time symptoms show, you're stuck with the car."
```

```
☐ Paint consistency (run your hand, feel for bumps = repainting = accident)
☐ Rust on body (doors, wheel wells, bottom of doors)
☐ Tire tread (>3mm ideal; <2mm = replace soon, RM400+)
☐ All lights work (headlights, taillights, brake lights, turn signals)
☐ Windows roll up/down smoothly
☐ Dents/scratches (photo for negotiation)

ENGINE BAY (3 mins):
☐ Oil dipstick (brown = good, black = dirty, foamy = water inside — WALK)
☐ Coolant (pink/green = normal, rusty brown = neglect)
☐ Battery (corroded terminals?)
☐ Belts/hoses (cracks visible?)
☐ Check for oil leaks (small spot OK, puddles = bad)

INTERIOR (5 mins):
☐ Smell (musty/moldy = flood — WALK)
☐ Seats (tears, stains, can you adjust?)
☐ Steering wheel (solid or loose?)
☐ Pedals (good resistance or mushy?)
☐ Floor mats (wet underneath?)
☐ Trunk (check same as interior)

ELECTRICAL (2 mins):
☐ Radio/stereo works
☐ Air con blows cold
☐ Power windows (all 4)
☐ Power locks
☐ Wipers spray water
☐ No warning lights on dashboard (ABS, engine, airbag should be OFF)

TEST DRIVE (15 mins):
☐ Start cold (should fire instantly, no rough idle)
☐ Transmission smooth (no clunking on gear changes)
☐ Steering doesn't vibrate at highway speed
☐ Brakes smooth (no squeaks, grinding)
☐ Acceleration smooth (no hesitation)
☐ Listen for noises (ticking, grinding, knocking = bad)
☐ Suspension over bumps (should feel smooth, not clunky)

Total time: ~40 minutes. Worth every second."

❌ Major rust on undercarriage (flood or accident)
```

## Recording Tips

- Phone or camera (doesn't need to be fancy)
- Quiet room (no background noise)
- Good lighting (natural is best)
- Lapel mic or earbuds mic (phone mic is fine)

**Production Flow:**
1. Read script once (get familiar)
2. Record (multiple takes if needed)
3. Edit (cut dead air, add cuts for the visuals mentioned)
4. Add text overlays (emphasize numbers, red flags)
5. Add B-roll (cars, inspection footage, Paqar app screenshots)

**Visuals to Include:**
- Paqar app screenshots (valuation lookup)
- JomCheck process
- Example car negotiation chart
- Red flag callouts with onscreen text

**Publishing:**
- YouTube channel (create if needed)
- 3x/week if possible (build momentum)
- Link to FAQ pages in video descriptions
- Pin top comment with links
